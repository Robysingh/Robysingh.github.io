Research & teaching software · IEEE publication · 2023

MUSIC and pattern-synthesis anti-jamming app

Presented at IEEE AP-S / USNC-URSI 2023, Portland

An interactive MATLAB application for teaching and researching adaptive arrays. You configure a rectangular array (rows, columns, spacing, carrier frequency), drop in one to four interfering signals or random ones, and the app estimates their directions with the MUSIC algorithm, computes null-steering weights, and animates the resulting pattern in polar, rectangular and 3D views.

I wrote the app and the null-steering algorithm, and paired them with a colleague's MUSIC estimator. The app exports its weights so they can be checked in full electromagnetic simulation.

GNSS anti-jamming · GPS L1

2×2 patch array for GPS

Designed and simulated a 2×2 patch antenna array at 1.575 GHz in CST, iterating through ground plane, slotted ground, reflector, cavity and frequency-selective-surface variants. Steering the phase of two elements against two reference elements placed a null about 40 dB deep in the chosen direction.

Drone anti-jamming · with the UK MoD

Automated backlobe suppression search

For a 2×2 drone antenna array, I wrote MATLAB scripts that sweep over a thousand phase and amplitude combinations against simulated pattern data and table every combination that produces a null at a given angle. Over six versions the tool went from a manual 8-angle check to a fully automated sweep from 80° to 280°.

MSc dissertation · Swansea University · 2025

Nova: a customisable robot companion for older adults and people with dementia

Mark: 87%User study: 10 participantsBuild cost: ≈ £135

Commercial robot pets like PARO and Joy for All are expensive and repeat the same behaviour, so users lose interest. I built Nova from scratch inside a £10 dog puppet: a robot dog whose responses the user chooses, including which sound it makes when you stroke its head or body, depending on your mood.

A Raspberry Pi 4 reads the user's facial expression and listens for its name; a Raspberry Pi Pico handles touch, motion and sound in real time, with touch sensors I built from resistors and the Pico's own pins. In a 10-person before-and-after study, negative feelings about emotional interaction with robots dropped significantly (NARS emotions subscale, p = .007), and participants valued the customisation most.

Invent for the Planet · 48 hours · 2025

Vapoura: passive saltwater cooling for data centres

Cooling is about half of a data centre's power draw. Our team designed a zero-energy unit that cools liquid loops with humid outdoor air and saltwater through a porous PTFE membrane, saving freshwater too. Three stacked units were estimated to cut a 130 kW site's power use by about 12%. Won the Royal Academy of Engineering's Most Innovative Engineering Idea award among 700+ students from 40+ universities.

  • Thermal design
  • Cost model ≈ $5,150 / unit
  • Team of 6
